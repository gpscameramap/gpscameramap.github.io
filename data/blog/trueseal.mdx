# TrueSeal: Enterprise-Grade, Self-Custodial Document Signing & Verification for Android

<Description>
In an era of AI-generated content, digital spoofing, and increasing regulatory scrutiny, establishing **mathematically provable authenticity and non-repudiation** for your digital documents is no longer optional. Whether you are an individual sharing sensitive contracts, a developer verifying data integrity, or a legal professional validating government-issued certificates, traditional "e-signatures" (often just flat image overlays) fall dangerously short of true security.

Enter **TrueSeal**, an enterprise-grade, self-custodial cryptographic signature and verification platform built natively for Android using Jetpack Compose, Room, and Firebase.

## What is TrueSeal?

TrueSeal empowers users to cryptographically sign, seal, and verify documents, PDFs, images, archives, and arbitrary binary data. By anchoring trust directly in local hardware-backed security modules rather than third-party SaaS servers, TrueSeal ensures that **you remain the sole custodian of your cryptographic identity**.

---

## 🔒 Core Cryptographic Architecture

TrueSeal is engineered from the ground up to offer zero-trust integrity, bulletproof privacy, and strict adherence to cryptographic standards.

### 1. Hardware-Backed Self-Custody & Asymmetric Signatures

* **Algorithm:** Implements the **NIST P-256 (secp256r1)** Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (`SHA256withECDSA`).
* **Hardware Isolation:** Private keys are generated and stored exclusively within the device’s local Android Keystore / Hardware Security Module (HSM). Private keys **never** transit network sockets, cloud backups, or third-party servers.

### 2. Universal DSC & eSign Verifier

TrueSeal goes beyond local signatures by featuring an automated verifier for standard PDF Digital Signature Certificates (DSC). It seamlessly inspects and validates:

* **Government & eSign Certificates:** Aadhaar eSign, e-PAN digital signatures, and Income Tax Return (ITR-V) DSCs.
* **Corporate & Standard PKCS#7 / PAdES:** Complete validation of standard certificate hierarchies, CA root anchors, and byte-range integrity.

### 3. Stream-Based Cryptographic Hashing

* Uses **FIPS 180-4 Secure Hash Standard (SHA-256)** digests calculated stream-by-stream, allowing TrueSeal to hash large files and archives efficiently without hitting memory limits or leaking unencrypted buffers.

### 4. Deterministic Vault Backup & Encryption

* **Vault Security:** The local private key vault is encrypted using **AES-256-GCM**, with keys derived via `PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256` utilizing 12,000 iterations and cryptographic salt.
* **12-Word Mnemonic Recovery:** Features a BIP-39 style deterministic 12-word mnemonic backup system accompanied by a mandatory pre-activation verification challenge to prevent user setup errors.

### 5. Real-Time Tamper Detection & Custom Watermarking

* **Instant Verification:** Offers immediate cross-user validation. Valid documents receive a prominent **Big Green Verified Badge**, while even a single-bit file alteration immediately triggers a stark **Red Cross Warning**.
* **Configurable Overlay:** Customize signature watermarks in app settings with Multi-line (default) or Single-line formats positioned across 6 locations (Bottom Right, Bottom Center, Bottom Left, Top Right, Top Center, Top Left).

## 🚀 Native System Integration ("Open With")

TrueSeal hooks directly into Android’s system-level file manager and share sheets, providing a frictionless workflow across your device:

* **Open with TrueSeal Verify:** Tap any PDF, signed file, or standalone certificate from your system file manager to immediately inspect certificate hierarchies, byte-range integrity, and signature validity.
* **TrueSeal Sign Document:** Select any image, document, or archive from native system storage to stamp and cryptographically sign it on the fly using your local keys.
