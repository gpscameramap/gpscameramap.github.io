# FieldCam: GPS Camera – Capture Location, Time, and Context Directly on Every Media File

<Description>
In fields like site inspection, environmental research, field engineering, real estate, and travel documentation, a simple photo often isn't enough. Without verified spatial and temporal context, proving *where* and *when* an image or video was taken requires tedious cross-referencing of EXIF metadata, manual logbooks, or secondary mapping tools.
</Description>

**FieldCam: GPS Camera** solves this challenge by burning precise location metadata, temporal data, and visual mapping directly into your media as you capture it—delivering effortless proof and clear documentation on every shot.

### Core Metadata Overlays

* **Precise GPS Coordinates:** Latitude and longitude coordinates burned directly onto the image frame.
* **Altitude & Compass Heading:** Real-time elevation tracking and cardinal direction indicators to log spatial orientation.
* **Reverse-Geocoded Address:** Converts raw coordinates into readable street addresses, cities, and regions on the fly.
* **Timestamping:** High-accuracy date and time logging for verifiable audit trails.
* **Integrated MiniMap:** Renders a visual map inset on the corner of the capture, providing immediate geographic context at a glance.

Data security and operational speed are paramount when working in the field. FieldCam processes both location telemetry and media overlays **100% on-device**:

* **Zero Cloud Dependence:** All rendering, coordinate math, address lookups, and video processing occur locally on your hardware.
